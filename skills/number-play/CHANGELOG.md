# Changelog

## number-play 0.1.0, with numplay 0.1.0 (2026-11-17)

First release.

- **Three tags.** Every finding is marked structure, convention or made, and every answer ends with a count of readings tried and landed.
- **Four jobs.** Play with numbers, check a day, write a post that counts itself, and settle a base-ten argument.
- **`numplay` 0.1.0.** `facts`, `collatz`, `day` and `count`, exact for whole numbers up to 2^64 - 1, with no dependencies.
- **Build from source.** No prebuilt executable is committed; `build.ps1` builds it and runs the tests.
